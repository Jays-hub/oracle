## 0. The one-line finding

A **warm, experienced, well-run, anti-software multi-unit operator** — the textbook halo/insider case.

not demand forecasting. The newsvendor-shaped domain (BOH perishable prep, waste, run-outs) belongs to

## 2. The two gating pulls

**A1 — saturation (lead confirm-or-kill).** Does a tool he *has* output per-item prep quantities, or
stop at covers/$?

> Toast's "Ask AI" gives him a **dollar/covers** projection: *"find notable local events near [Depot
> BBQ] tonight… net sales between basically $2,000."* It stops at $, not "make N portions." **BUT** he
> also volunteered, unprompted: *"I have seen some good uses of Claude… people pushing all of their POS
> and reservation data to cloud… having it kind of learn how much of an item you're likely to need over
> the course of the next [period]. Maybe for ordering reasons… but I don't know that I fully trust [it]."*

→ **The confirm-or-kill lands MIXED.** Off-the-shelf (Toast) stops at $ — confirms the gap. But
per-item quantity forecasting is **already a known, DIY'd concept** in his world, and the barrier he
names is not *absence* — it's **trust**. That reframes the wedge: the hard part isn't producing the
number, it's earning belief in it.

**A12 / A13 — data access.** POS = **Toast** (known API); he also has reservation data and detailed
product mixes, and pulled Toast up live. **Willing and capable in principle.** But **no export was
secured, and the model-relevant BOH data lives with Wes, not Reid.** Access is *plausible, unproven.*
